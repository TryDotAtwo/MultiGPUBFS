# Matrix Cayley workloads

## Symmetric groups

The first non-toy family is the regular Cayley graph of `S_n` represented by
`n x n` permutation matrices over modulus two. The inverse-closed generating
set is the cycle `(1 2 ... n)`, its inverse, and `(1 2)`. This exact three-move
family is studied as a symmetric-group Cayley graph in
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08715>. The expected order is `n!`.

Independent host verification uses a permutation-group Schreier-Sims order
calculation, not the matrix BFS implementation. Frozen results:

| Group | Order | Macro weights 1..10 | Cumulative K10 | Macro digest V1 |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| S8 | 40,320 | 3, 6, 12, 23, 44, 80, 142, 247, 411, 662 | 1,630 | `02220649b1f7c272ca1bbcf4bbb1eb24727913388fc58ea4814e506ad78320eb` |
| S12 | 479,001,600 | 3, 6, 12, 24, 48, 90, 168, 314, 572, 1,033 | 2,270 | `17fb246bf0e30a8a8c33616a2f8f5563bc7cca707a2d5c740137daf035018141` |

S8 is the complete correctness and archive/replay gate. S12 is the sustained
throughput, capacity and Parquet-streaming workload; no partial S12 run may be
reported as a completed graph.

## Transposition-tree family

Cayley graphs of `S_n` generated by transpositions corresponding to a tree are
a separate meaningful family with different duplicate/locality structure; see
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.3114>. Adjacent-path and star trees will be distinct
frozen manifests rather than aliases for the three-generator family above.

Additional alternating and finite-linear groups remain outside the release
matrix until their exact representation, inverse map and order are independently
verified. A familiar group name alone is not accepted as a benchmark manifest.
