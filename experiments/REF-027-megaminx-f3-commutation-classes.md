# REF-027: current Megaminx F3 commutation classes

Date: 2026-08-28

Status: pass after one formatting-only failed gate

## Question

Do the 3,008 candidate-record convergence extras while forming current
Megaminx `F3` require additional length-three word equalities, or are they all
consequences of the 36 commuting face pairs identified by REF-026?

## Why candidate records are insufficient

REF-026 counted 9,216 forward records from 408 unique `F2` states. But 144 of
those states have two shortest words. Once `F2` is deduplicated, a candidate
record remembers one parent state, not every shortest word reaching it.

Therefore REF-027 independently enumerates all

```text
24 * 23 * 23 = 12,696
```

non-backtracking words of length three, evaluates them with the full 120-entry
state action, and retains exactly those whose endpoints have distance three.

## Commutation quotient

Two words are placed in one class only by repeatedly swapping adjacent moves
whose actual permutations commute. For length-three words the probe enumerates
the complete finite swap closure and chooses its lexicographically least word
as a normal form.

This quotient uses no inferred braid or power rewrite. It contains exactly the
local consequences of the commuting move pairs under adjacent swaps.

## Exact result

```text
unique F3 states                         6,208
candidate records from unique F2         9,216
all shortest length-three words         12,384
word occurrences beyond one per state    6,176
classes after commuting swaps             6,208
extras remaining after quotient               0
```

Every `F3` endpoint has exactly one commutation class. Hence every equality
among its shortest length-three words is generated by adjacent swaps of known
commuting moves.

The 6,176 word-level extras split into two implementation-visible stages:

```text
hidden when alternate F2 histories become one parent state: 12,384-9,216=3,168
visible as convergent F2 candidate records:                  9,216-6,208=3,008
total word extras:                                           6,176.
```

Thus the previously open 3,008 count requires no additional geodesic
length-three relation.

## Shortest-word multiplicities

| Number of shortest words | F3 states |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 2,880 |
| 2 | 960 |
| 3 | 2,208 |
| 6 | 160 |

The values `2`, `3`, and `6` are consistent with the number of linear orders
allowed by commuting letters. In particular, multiplicity six is the complete
permutation set of three pairwise commuting moves. REF-027 proves the common
commutation class, while this linear-extension language is the interpretation.

## What zero remainder does and does not prove

It proves:

- no two distance-three words for one `F3` state lie in different commutation
  classes;
- all shortest-word and candidate convergence through `F3` is explained by
  the known commuting move relation;
- the order-five relation contributes same-level `F2` edges, not new `F3`
  geodesic classes.

It does not prove:

- that the 36 commutators and 12 power relations present the whole group;
- absence of a short relator whose halves are not both geodesic length-three
  words;
- anything about relations first visible beyond `F3`;
- GPU locality or performance.
