# REF-028: first non-trace Megaminx geodesic equalities

Date: 2026-08-28

Status: pass after two informative failed gates

## Question

What is the first current Megaminx BFS layer at which two shortest words for
one state need more than adjacent swaps of commuting generator permutations?

REF-027 exhaustively proved that no such equality exists through `F3`.
At length four there are only

```text
24 * 23^3 = 292,008
```

non-backtracking words, so REF-028 deliberately uses one final simple full-word
enumeration rather than prematurely inventing a compact propagation scheme.

## Exact result

```text
unique F4 states                         90,144
candidate records from unique F3        139,248
all shortest length-four words          274,224
classes after adjacent commuting swaps   90,624
classes beyond one per state                 480
states with more than one class              480
maximum classes for one state                  2
```

Therefore `F4` is the first layer with geodesic equality not generated by the
static commutation relation on the original 24-letter move alphabet.

The word extras decompose as

```text
274,224 - 90,144 = 184,080 total extras
183,600                        explained by adjacent commuting swaps
    480                        left after that quotient.
```

Candidate records expose a different projection:

```text
274,224 - 139,248 = 134,976 histories already hidden by parent-state dedup
139,248 -  90,144 =  49,104 convergent records visible while forming F4.
```

These counts answer different questions. In particular, `49,104` visible
candidate extras must not be mistaken for the `184,080` alternate shortest
words or for the `480` additional relation classes.

## Classification of all 480 pairs

Every one of the 480 cross-class pairs has one of the two cyclic forms

```text
g h k h^-1 = h k h^-1 g
h k h^-1 g = g h k h^-1.
```

Thus all 480 are commutators of a generator `g` with a conjugated generator
`h k h^-1`. Example:

```text
-B -BL -L BL = -BL -L BL -B.
```

The equality is exact under the full 120-entry permutation state. The
classifier checks both cyclic orientations and checks the inverse index
explicitly; it does not infer the pattern from move-name spelling.

## Interpretation

Static trace equivalence says that two adjacent letters may swap when their
generator permutations commute. The new equality says something richer: the
composite group element `h k h^-1` commutes with `g`. In a permutation model,
conjugation transports the support of `k`; it does not make the meaning or
legality of the generator depend on the current BFS vertex. The relation is
visible in the group action but not as an adjacent swap of original letters.

For BFS this separates three objects:

- a state remains the correct unit for exact distance and visited dedup;
- the shortest-path DAG may merge histories from distinct static trace
  classes;
- a canonicalizer based only on a fixed generator commutation matrix ceases to
  represent all shortest-path equivalence at depth four.

This does not make state dedup incorrect. It shows only that path multiplicity
and relation structure contain information that ordinary state-only BFS is
designed to discard.

Equating two length-four geodesics gives an expanded length-eight commutator
relator. REF-028 establishes this finite scoped fact; it does not claim a full
group presentation or that every later relation is generated by these
commutators.
